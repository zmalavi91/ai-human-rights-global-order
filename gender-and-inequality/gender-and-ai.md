# Gender, Inequality, and Artificial Intelligence

## Research Focus

AI systems can reproduce or intensify existing social inequalities. Gender-sensitive research examines how technology affects women, men, gender minorities, and communities differently.

---

## Key Research Areas

- Gender bias in training data.
- Automated employment discrimination.
- Gender-based online violence.
- Facial-recognition disparities.
- Digital exclusion.
- Surveillance of women activists.
- Political participation.
- Health-data privacy.
- Reproductive-data governance.
- Unequal access to digital infrastructure.
- Gender and AI labour markets.
- Gender representation in technology institutions.

---

## Analytical Questions

1. Are women and gender minorities adequately represented in the data?
2. Does the system reproduce existing gender stereotypes?
3. Does the system generate different error rates across groups?
4. Can gender be inferred without meaningful consent?
5. Does automated decision-making affect employment opportunities?
6. How does online violence restrict political participation?
7. Are women involved in the design and governance of AI?
8. Does the system protect bodily and personal autonomy?
9. How do gender, caste, race, class, and disability intersect?
10. Are gender-sensitive remedies available?

---

## Intersectionality

AI impacts should be examined through multiple and overlapping structures of inequality, including:

- Gender.
- Caste.
- Race.
- Class.
- Disability.
- Religion.
- Language.
- Migration status.
- Geography.
- Age.

An intersectional approach avoids treating women or other groups as internally uniform.

---

## Policy Recommendations

- Require gender-sensitive impact assessments.
- Include diverse communities in system design.
- Test outcomes across demographic groups.
- Protect against gender-based digital violence.
- Improve access to digital education and infrastructure.
- Establish clear remedies for automated discrimination.
- Strengthen privacy protections for sensitive personal data.
- Support women’s participation in technology governance.
- Collect equality data responsibly and securely.
