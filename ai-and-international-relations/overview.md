# Artificial Intelligence and International Relations

## Research Focus

Artificial intelligence is becoming an important factor in international politics. It influences:

- Foreign-policy analysis.
- Strategic competition.
- International security.
- Diplomatic decision-making.
- Military technology.
- Economic statecraft.
- Global governance.
- International communication.

## Core Questions

1. How does AI change the distribution of international power?
2. How are states using AI in foreign policy and security?
3. How does AI affect strategic autonomy?
4. What role do technology companies play in global politics?
5. How can international institutions govern high-risk AI systems?
6. What are the consequences for developing countries?
7. How can AI affect international human-rights norms?

## Analytical Approach

Research should connect:

- Technology.
- Power.
- Institutions.
- International norms.
- Security.
- Human rights.
- Global inequality.
- Accountability.

## Key Caution

AI should not be treated as politically neutral. Its effects depend on who designs, controls, deploys, regulates, and benefits from the technology.
